Why Philantree

Wanting to help is easy. Knowing where to start shouldn't be hard.

Many people want to volunteer but don't know where to begin. Many nonprofits need help but struggle to reach the right people at the right time.

Hard to find

Opportunities are scattered across websites, social media, flyers, and word of mouth, so finding one that fits your schedule and interests takes real effort.

Hard to coordinate

Nonprofits often juggle email, spreadsheets, sign-up sheets, and group chats just to schedule volunteers and share updates.

Hard to stay involved

Volunteers who don't feel connected or recognized drift away, and documenting service hours for school or work is often a hassle.

How it will work

From "I want to help" to showing up

The first version of Philantree focuses on one simple, reliable path between nonprofits and volunteers.

  1. Nonprofits post opportunities

    Date, location, what's involved, and how many people are needed.

  2. Volunteers discover and sign up

    Browse nearby opportunities that match your interests and schedule.

  3. Everyone shows up prepared

    Both sides have the details they need to coordinate.

  4. Impact is recorded

    Hours and experiences are logged so volunteers can come back and nonprofits can improve.
